Sports injury treatment at the Joint and Bone Institute of California

If you suffer a sports injury involving the joints or bones, reach out to the Joint and Bone Institute of California at (661) 544-3352. We have successfully treated patients with all different types of bone and joint problems caused by sports injuries.

Sports medicine treatments and procedures are tailored to each individual patient’s unique injury to hasten the restoration of both movement and functionality. Even if you are not a high-level athlete — perhaps you hurt yourself in a neighborhood pickup game or a school activity — our team is happy to treat your sports injury as appropriate.

Non-surgical sports injury treatment first.

The majority of sports injuries do not require surgery. We exhaust non-surgical options before ever considering an operation. Non-operative sports injury treatments include, but are not limited to, ongoing management and injections administered through ultrasound guidance, such as:

  • Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) injections
  • Hyaluronic acid injections
  • Steroid injections

If surgery does become necessary, a procedure such as joint arthroplasty with an artificial joint may be used to replace a joint that has suffered damage from a sports injury.

When to seek sports injury care.

If you experience any of the following after a sports injury, reach out to us right away so we can get the ball rolling on your sports injury treatment:

  • Bruising
  • Significant pain
  • Lack of movement
  • Angulation of a limb or joint
  • Skin discoloration

We’ll do everything in our power to relieve your pain, restore your motion, and help you get back onto the field of play as soon as possible.
