Fracture care at the Joint and Bone Institute of California

With the proper care, fractures will gradually heal without causing significant pain along the way. If you know or suspect you have a fracture at any body site, reach out to our medical professionals today by dialing (661) 544-3352 to schedule an appointment where we discuss your fracture care.

The majority of fractures are treated in a manner that immobilizes the bone so it can heal naturally. Such immobilization is accomplished with a cast, a splint, a soft brace, or possibly even a boot. In certain cases, the broken bone has to be set in place or returned to alignment as part of your fracture care.

Personalized fracture treatment.

Our fracture care specialists understand the subtle nuances of fracture setting that help a compromised bone heal properly. Our team will also determine whether surgery is necessary to keep the bone in position. The best treatment for your unique fracture hinges on the type of fracture as well as the specific bone or bones fractured. Fracture correction can occur through several different approaches:

  • Traction alignment
  • Casting and bracing
  • Setting the bone back into alignment
  • External fixation with metal pins and wires

When to seek fracture care.

If you know or suspect a bone is broken, prompt fracture care gives the bone the best chance to heal properly. Don’t wait — the sooner a fracture is evaluated and immobilized, the smoother the recovery tends to be.

The right fracture care depends on the type of break and the bone involved. Our specialists tailor every treatment plan to help your bone heal properly.
