01 · Post-Op Service

Injections

After certain procedures, targeted injections can help manage inflammation, ease pain, and support the healing process. At JBI, injections are used thoughtfully as one part of your overall recovery plan — not a one-size-fits-all fix.

When you might benefit

  • Persistent swelling or inflammation around the surgical area
  • Localized pain that’s slowing your therapy progress
  • Difficulty regaining range of motion during rehabilitation
  • A goal of reducing reliance on oral pain medication

What to expect

Injections are a quick, in-office procedure. Your provider will explain what is being injected and why, and most patients return to normal activity soon after. We monitor how you respond and adjust your recovery plan accordingly.

Injections are one tool in a broader recovery plan — your provider will recommend them only when they’re likely to help.

02 · Post-Op Service

Wound & Incision Care

Proper care of your surgical incision is one of the most important parts of a smooth recovery. We give you clear, simple instructions and check the site at every visit to make sure it’s healing as expected.

When this matters

  • After any procedure that involves a surgical incision
  • Dressing changes and guidance on keeping the area protected
  • Questions about showering, bathing, or getting the site wet
  • Any redness, drainage, warmth, or swelling around the incision

What to expect

You’ll keep the area clean and dry as instructed, and we’ll inspect the incision at each follow-up visit. We’ll guide you on activity that protects the site and walk you through the signs of healthy healing versus warning signs to watch for.

Call us right away if you notice increasing redness, drainage, swelling, a foul odor, or a fever — these can be early signs of infection.

03 · Post-Op Service

Pain Management

Comfort is essential to healing. We build a pain-management plan tailored to your procedure and your needs, using the safest and most effective combination of approaches to keep you moving.

When this matters

  • In the days and weeks following your surgery
  • Pain that limits your sleep or your therapy progress
  • Transitioning safely off of stronger medications
  • A preference for a non-opioid-focused approach

What to expect

Your plan may combine several methods — medication, ice, positioning, therapy, and injections when appropriate. You’ll get clear instructions on timing and dosing, plus regular check-ins so we can adjust as you recover.

Our aim is effective relief with the lowest necessary medication — and reducing reliance on opioids whenever possible.

04 · Post-Op Service

Follow-Up & Suture Removal

Scheduled follow-up visits let us track your healing, remove sutures or staples at the right time, and adjust your recovery plan as you progress.

When this matters

  • Every patient after a surgical procedure
  • Suture or staple removal once the incision is ready
  • Imaging or progress checks to confirm you’re healing well
  • Clearance to return to work, driving, or activity

What to expect

Your first follow-up is typically within one to two weeks. Suture or staple removal is quick and comfortable. We’ll review your incision and range of motion, and map out the next steps in your recovery.

Keeping your follow-up appointments is one of the simplest ways to protect your result — even if you’re feeling great.
