From urgent care to orthopedics.

Kelly Guzman began her career in primary care and urgent care. While working in urgent care, she met Dr. Karan Srivastava when he came in to lead a splinting workshop. After taking time off following the birth of her daughter, she was ready to return to work — and an opportunity in orthopedics came up. She joined the Joint & Bone Institute of California in 2025.

What she enjoys most about orthopedics is helping patients get back to doing the things they love. Whether it’s walking without pain, returning to work, or getting back to a sport, it’s rewarding to see that improvement.

“Patient care means listening first and making sure patients leave with a plan they understand and feel comfortable with.”

Education & training.

Kelly earned a Master of Science in Physician Assistant Studies, building on an undergraduate foundation in the biological sciences. Before moving into orthopedics, she gained hands-on clinical experience in urgent care and primary care.

A patient-first approach.

Kelly describes her approach as patient-first, reassuring, and responsive. She believes good care starts with listening — understanding what matters to each patient before building a plan together.

Her goal is for every patient to leave feeling heard, understood, and confident that they have a clear plan moving forward.

Outside the clinic.

Away from work, Kelly stays active — she runs, lifts, and teaches Lagree — and she loves to read. Most of all, she’s enjoying motherhood.
