— About the Practice

Care that starts with understanding.

At Joint & Bone Institute, our philosophy revolves around educating patients — giving you the knowledge and tools to determine which treatment best serves you. Each patient is different, and each patient has unique individual needs.

We strive to clearly explain what is causing your musculoskeletal symptoms and what can be done to solve your orthopedic issues. Through this teamwork and personalized care, we are committed to delivering a positive outcome for you.

Dr. Srivastava welcomes new and returning patients to our offices across the Central Valley. From minimally invasive procedures to more extensive treatments, we offer a wide range of services designed to help patients return to healthy, active lives.

— Mission & Vision

What we're working toward.

Our Vision

The model orthopedic practice

To be the model orthopedic practice — providing compassionate orthopedic care through innovation, technology, and evidence-based medicine, so patients can live a healthy, pain-free lifestyle.

Our Mission

Educate, explain, and partner

To educate patients on what is causing their symptoms, explain the different treatment options so patients can choose the care that best suits their needs, and work as a team to improve their quality of life.
